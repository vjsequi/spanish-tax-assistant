# Project glossary

| Term | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| Tax year | The income period being reviewed; first scope is 2025, distinct from its 2026 filing campaign. |
| Jurisdiction | The supported tax regime and region; first scope is common-regime Spanish residents in the Balearics. |
| Case | One taxpayer's review for one tax year, with authorised evidence and a version history. |
| Fact | A typed value extracted from evidence or confirmed by a person, with provenance and certainty state. |
| Conflict | Two facts that cannot both be used without resolving their relationship or authority. |
| Treatment | A supported classification, deduction or calculation, backed by a versioned rule. |
| Review packet | Evidence, established facts, missing information and proposed return-field changes for human review. |
| Proposed change | An uncommitted amendment to a draft field with its facts, rule, calculation and source references. |
| Corpus | The reviewed, versioned official material available to retrieval for the selected year and region. |
| Scope referral | A specific explanation that a case or treatment needs support beyond the declared product scope. |
| Independent review | Judgement by a qualified tax professional who checks the source-derived expected treatment. |
| Recorded demo | Reviewed synthetic application results presented without live inference. |
