# Candidate deduction feasibility research

**Researched 21 October 2026; not a validated rule set.** The owner subsequently required reviewed branches of both deductions for the first complete release. This brief records earlier source findings; the [pinned detailed specification](../requirements/tax-2025.md) now carries the source versions, rule contracts, field mapping and unresolved questions. No personal eligibility judgement, numeric implementation or reviewed case labels exist. Source retention is complete for this review set; professional applicability and calculation review remain pending.

## Evidence and dependency implications

| Candidate | Facts the review needs to establish | Product implication |
|---|---|---|
| Habitual-home rent | Qualifying personal/family circumstances, tenancy and occupation, actual payments and payer/contract attribution, household housing rights, relevant taxable bases and other housing-deduction entitlement | A lease and receipts alone do not establish eligibility. Household facts can matter even for an individual return. |
| Childcare/conciliation | Child and service category, relevant dates, employment periods, payments, other entitled persons, family circumstances, applicable taxable bases and incompatible treatments; caregiver registration for that branch | Receipt extraction must be followed by condition checks and time/entitlement attribution. |

These evidence proposals are inferred from the [AEAT 2025 rent manual](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-illes-balears/arrendamiento-vivienda-habitual.html) and [AEAT 2025 childcare manual](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-illes-balears/gastos-descendientes-acogidos-menores-seis-conciliacion.html). They are not an exclusive statutory list of acceptable proof. The manuals use general plus savings taxable bases for the income condition; gross salary is not a substitute. Unknown income dependencies must remain unresolved in a partial review.

## Explicit questions for independent review

1. **Rent household condition.** The practical manual's “Precisiones” applies the other-home condition across the family unit for individual and joint filing. The [completion help, requirements item 2](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/eu_es/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-ayuda-presentacion/irpf-2025/10-cumplimentacion-irpf-deducciones-autonomicas/10_4-illes-balears/10_4_12-arrendamiento-vivienda-habitual.html) contains a narrower individual-filing sentence. Preserve both source versions and obtain a source-derived resolution; do not choose the more permissive reading automatically.
2. **Childcare multi-child cap/proration.** The [completion help, amount and proration sections](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-ayuda-presentacion/irpf-2025/10-cumplimentacion-irpf-deducciones-autonomicas/10_4-illes-balears/10_4_18-gastos-relativos-descendientes-acogidos-anos.html) describes a per-child cap. The practical manual describes an annual cap and different multi-child proration wording. Do not infer a general multi-child calculation from the single-child example.
3. **Other benefits and temporal applicability.** The inspected passages do not settle all employer-paid/subsidised-fee or national-benefit interactions. Refer those branches. Verify the applicable 2025 wording and accrual circumstances, rather than use today's consolidated law without a date check.

The statutory references identified by AEAT are articles 3 bis and 6 bis of the [Balearics consolidated tax provisions, dated version](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-6925&p=20250724&tn=1). The two articles and later 2025 amendments require treatment-specific verification before rule freezing; a single dated consolidation must not be assumed to cover every amendment. Relevant family definitions are in the [Balearics family-support law](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-13193). Statutory references were identified in the research; this brief does not assert that a complete legal cross-check is finished.

- A childcare example with one child throughout the relevant year, clear educational-centre invoices/payments and employment periods.
